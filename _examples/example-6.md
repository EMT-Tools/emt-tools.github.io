<p>This example demonstrates the stability of a PI controlled system using a simple second order plant. Changing the Ki value of the integrator can make the closed loop system either stable or unstable. As Ki is reduced, the integral action becomes stronger and the response becomes increasingly oscillatory. The example demonstrates the transition from a damped response to sustained oscillations and eventually to growing instability. </p>
