<p>This example compares five commonly used filters from the PSCAD Master Library, such as the Real Pole and Differential Pole filters, with equivalent open model implementations. The filters are presented in the Master Library using Laplace domain transfer functions, which provide a convenient and compact way of expressing their mathematical behaviour. </p>
<p>The open models reproduce the same filter equations using transparent PSCAD and EMTDC implementations. Their outputs are compared directly with the corresponding Master Library filters to demonstrate equivalent behaviour while providing full visibility into how each filter is implemented. </p>
