<p>This is not a single PSCAD example, but an evolving collection of custom components I have developed for my own projects, testing, and general experimentation. I will continue to refine existing components and add new ones over time as the library grows. </p>
<p>The collection includes logic blocks, meters, displays, and a variety of miscellaneous tools ranging from genuinely useful to occasionally questionable. The goal is to provide practical building blocks that can be reused, modified, or simply explored to better understand custom component development in PSCAD. </p>
