Free PSCAD Resources

This site is a growing, community driven collection of downloadable PSCAD examples covering model development, controls, inverter based resources (IBRs), renewable generation, and modern power system simulation. We welcome submissions from the power systems community, with the goal of building the largest collection of freely available PSCAD examples.

Looking for more?

Mathias founded EMT-Tools with the vision of building the largest online collection of freely downloadable PSCAD examples, supported by contributions from the global power systems community.

With over 14 years of industry experience using PSCAD on a daily basis, including 11 years working directly with its development team, he has gained an in-depth understanding of the software, its capabilities, and its complexities. Through EMT-Tools, he aims to share this knowledge, promote collaboration, and make valuable PSCAD resources freely accessible to the wider engineering community.

Follow Mathias on LinkedIn for updates on new PSCAD example releases, practical tips, model development techniques, debugging lessons, and insights gained from real-world simulation experience.
