Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 10, 2026

At EMT Tools, accessible from https://emt-tools.com, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document contains types of information that is collected and recorded by EMT Tools and how we use it.

1. Information We Collect

Because our website is a static platform, we collect very limited personal information. The only personal data we collect is the information you voluntarily provide when using our Contact Form. This includes:

  • Your Name
  • Your Email Address
  • The contents of the message you send us

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect in various ways, including to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain our website and model audit tools
  • Improve, personalize, and expand our services
  • Understand and analyze how you use our website
  • Communicate with you, either directly or through one of our partners, including for customer service and support
  • Process your inquiries regarding our PSCAD collection and subscription services

3. Third-Party Services

Our contact form is powered by a third-party service (Web3Forms) which securely routes your message to our email inbox. We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personally identifiable information to outside parties.

4. Log Files

EMT Tools follows a standard procedure of using log files. These files log visitors when they visit websites. The information collected by log files include internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date and time stamp, referring/exit pages, and possibly the number of clicks. These are not linked to any information that is personally identifiable.

5. Consent

By using our website, you hereby consent to our Privacy Policy and agree to its terms.
